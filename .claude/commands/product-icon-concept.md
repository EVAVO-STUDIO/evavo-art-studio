# Product icon concept

Create product-specific launcher icon concepts, not a finished raw AI export.

Read the target `product-craft.json`. Extract the human job, audience, emotional tone, physical/product cues, anti-references and platform family. Produce three to five concept territories that differ in metaphor and silhouette, not merely colour. Avoid wordmarks, letters, tiny linework, generic gradients, stock spectacles, robot heads and AI sparkle motifs.

For the selected territory, create a layered raster master at 2048×2048 or larger with deliberate lighting/material decisions and generous mask-safe spacing. Record source hashes, rights, generation method and human selection/refinement notes. Hand geometry and optical issues to Vector Studio. Mark the result `concept`, never `production`, until Product Craft validates the final identity manifest.
