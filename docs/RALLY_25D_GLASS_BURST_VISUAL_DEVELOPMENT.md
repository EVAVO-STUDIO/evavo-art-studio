# Glass Burst visual-development jobs

Glass Burst is the vehicle glazing failure slice that follows Skid Smoke in the Rally 2.5D production graph. It is visually distinct from Gravel Spray, metal debris, sparks, engine smoke and fire.

The Art contract compiles twelve independent transparent source images covering the impact star, laminated front-glass response, tempered side and rear response, low and high impulse, crosswind, four runtime fragment roles and late decay. Every job is one `2048 x 2048` PNG with its own output path, dependency list, idempotency key and SHA-256 identity.

Laminated windscreen flakes remain flatter and more connected. Tempered side and rear glass breaks into smaller blunt fragments. Larger chunks stay close to the impact origin while fine glitter decays quickly. The specification rejects blood, gore, fire, whole vehicles, road backgrounds, contact sheets, knife-like fantasy shards and automatic creative approval.

The compiler prepares provider-ready metadata only. It does not render, approve, alter image bytes, assemble sheets, mutate downstream repositories, commit generated art, deploy or publish. Exact generated PNG bytes and named-human approval remain required before 3D production.
