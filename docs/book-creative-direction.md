# Book creative-direction intelligence

The versioned contract is:

`evavo_art_book_creative_direction_v1`

This compiler sits between Writing Studio narrative evidence and Art Studio candidate production. It does not tell an image provider to “be creative.” It chooses subjects, visual territories, composition, print process, mark grammar, negative space and spoiler boundaries from the actual book.

## Evidence before aesthetics

Each direction is bound to manuscript locations, approved evidence, rights evidence, themes, motifs, settings, character states and scene candidates. A style adjective or genre label cannot replace that evidence.

The compiler rejects:

- named-creator imitation;
- branded-franchise transfer;
- vague provider buzzwords such as “masterpiece,” “8k,” “epic cinematic” or “trending on ArtStation”;
- unapproved rights evidence;
- generated lettering, labels, logos or pseudo-text;
- cover routes that expose major or ending spoilers;
- concept routes that are merely cosmetic variations.

## Genre and purpose intelligence

Profiles cover literary, historical, horror, mythic, project-owned grimdark fantasy, science fiction, crime, romance, children’s books, memoir, documentary, technical and reference books, graphic novels, pulp, poetry, cookbooks, academic works and custom genres.

The same genre is treated differently for a front cover, full wrap, interior illustration, spot illustration, map, diagram or ornament. Covers favour spoiler-safe symbols, environmental pressure, relationships and consequences. Interior art may use later scenes when permitted. Graphic novels add sequential rhythm and editable lettering space. Technical and reference art prioritises systems, comparison and label-ready geometry.

## Multiple concept territories

The compiler creates two to four materially different territories selected from:

- material symbol;
- environment as pressure;
- relational tension;
- consequence before or after peak action;
- systems cutaway;
- sequential rhythm.

Selected territories must use different route kinds and composition archetypes. Each territory becomes a complete, fingerprinted `evavo_book_art_brief` that can be passed into the existing production work order and candidate-set pipeline. The compiler itself does not call a provider or choose final artwork.

## Composition

Composition is a narrative decision rather than a stock poster template. The plan defines focal hierarchy, subject scale, framing, action vectors, relational distance, depth, value grouping, physically motivated light and title or author quiet areas.

It explicitly rejects floating-head montages, generic heroes on ridges, glowing portals, centred swords with symmetrical ornaments, anonymous embracing couples, wet-alley detective silhouettes, stock nebulae and other genre-default shortcuts unless exact manuscript evidence makes an otherwise similar element specific and necessary.

## Material-specific mark grammar

Old-school linework is not a scratch filter. The compiler supports relief engraving, intaglio, scratchboard, brush-and-pen halftone, linocut, lithographic tone, duotone, risograph, black-only work, graphic-novel ink, technical plate, cartographic linework and ornamental print.

The resulting plan requires:

- primary contours for silhouette and structural load;
- secondary contours for plane breaks and construction;
- tertiary marks only where material, light or evidence changes;
- hatching that follows form, material and light;
- controlled black masses and reproducible fine lines;
- hand-resolved transitions and controlled irregularity.

It rejects plastic surfaces, uniform micro-detail, cloned texture stamps, global scratch overlays, orange-blue grading, arbitrary rim light, floating particles, fake writing, meaningless runes and generic movie-poster hierarchy.

## Production authority

The compiler performs no provider call, candidate selection, promotion, Book-use binding or publication. Generated artwork remains text-free. Docs Suite retains editable typography, lettering, labels, captions, alt text and reading order.

Every produced candidate still requires technical and print-craft QA, independent visual consensus, complete pairwise originality comparison, Docs Suite creative-quality approval, governed selection and promotion, and Book-use binding before layout or release.

## Controlled first production use

The first live use should target a non-publication edition. Confirm that each selected territory has exact manuscript locations, that different territories really use different compositions, and that the provider returns separate artifacts rather than a contact sheet or grid. Any weak, generic, stale or incomplete candidate is revised or regenerated. It is never padded with a fallback result or promoted because it merely looks polished.
