# Skid Smoke visual-development jobs

Skid Smoke is the asphalt-only tyre-smoke slice that follows Gravel Spray in the Rally 2.5D production graph. It is deliberately distinct from warm dirt dust, dark engine smoke, fire and impact sparks.

The Art contract compiles ten independent transparent source images covering the contact puff, low- and high-speed slip, brake lock-up, left and right crosswind response, three runtime sprite roles and late decay. Every job is one `2048 × 2048` PNG with a deterministic hash, dependency list, idempotency key, working path and master path.

The effect remains grounded at the rear wheel contact patches, travels vehicle-rearward, preserves a cool white-grey temperature and separates into compact core, directional wisp and low haze layers. Contact sheets, road backgrounds, baked vehicle silhouettes, opaque smoke walls, black engine smoke and warm dirt dust are rejected.

The compiler prepares provider-ready jobs only. It does not execute a provider, approve a candidate, mutate image bytes, assemble sheets, alter downstream repositories, commit generated media, deploy or publish. Exact rendered bytes and named-human approval remain required before 3D or runtime production.
