# Road-sign visual-development jobs

The Forest Rally road-sign kit is the next playable support asset after the timber bridge. It compiles ten independent one-image jobs for three original, non-text sign variants: chevron, hazard diamond and route arrow.

The contract preserves one shared post, brace, fastener and material family while keeping every variant, orthographic source, material reference and damage reference independently reviewable. It forbids real authority branding, place names, generated text, contact sheets and multi-prop compositions.

Each job has a deterministic hash, idempotency key, dependency list, unique PNG path and closed provider/approval/repository authority. Rendered candidates still require named-human approval and exact-byte admission before 3D production.
